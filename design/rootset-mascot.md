# Rootset elephant mascot

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. The friendly elephant is the identity reference for the sad and bucket variants. All three assets have transparent backgrounds and work on light and dark surfaces.

The production assets in `apps/rootset-web/public/mascot/` are 512 × 512 WebP files. Each is about 35 KiB; no image-generation request runs in the application.

- `elephant.webp`: friendly wave for empty database and table lists.
- `elephant-sad.webp`: sad expression for missing pages and unexpected errors.
- `elephant-bucket.webp`: holding an empty bucket for unavailable backup storage.

## Identity prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Rootset application mascot for empty states, isolated transparent PNG.
Primary request: Create one charming dark forest green baby elephant mascot, a polished soft 3D character in the same friendly family as a rounded little app mascot. Full body, oversized rounded head and large floppy elephant ears, small plump body, short rounded legs and feet, two short rounded arms, a clearly recognizable gently curved elephant trunk. Big expressive glossy dark eyes with warm highlights, tiny friendly smile. No clothes, no logo, no accessories, no text. Keep elephant anatomy clear with only one trunk and two ears; no tusks needed.
Style: premium soft matte 3D collectible, smooth subtly velvety surface, simple broad forms, warm approachable expression, restrained details that read at 160px. Deep forest green body around #2c5d43, slightly lighter sage green inner ears, subtle warm neutral lighting to preserve green on a dark UI. Not glossy plastic, no realistic wrinkled skin, no fur.
Composition: one centered full-body elephant, three-quarter view slightly turned while looking at viewer, a small friendly wave with one arm, other arm relaxed, trunk gently curled. Square canvas, entire ears, feet and wave within frame, character occupies about 80% of frame. Stable standing pose, no scene, no floor or cast floor shadow. Genuinely transparent background with alpha, clean silhouette edges, not a checkerboard.
Avoid: backgrounds, scenery, borders, lettering, watermark, charts, databases, other characters, photographic realism, gloomy coloring. This will be the identity reference for two later pose variants.
